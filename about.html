The Studio

Art Bhagyashree
Studio

Art Bhagyashree Studio is the creative studio of artist Bhagyashree Deshpande, specialising in large-scale, hyper-realistic Rangoli installations, live cultural performances, workshops and public art experiences.

Bhagyashree Deshpande is a Pune-based Rangoli artist who completed her education in Applied Arts. She creates immersive works in both traditional and realistic portrait Rangoli, and has developed a distinctive signature style for representing themes and stories through art.

She has performed at festivals across India and at international art festivals in the USA, UK and South Korea — and has been part of two world-record Rangoli projects.

Our Vision

Preserving a Living Tradition

To preserve, promote, and reimagine the traditional art of Indian Rangoli through world-class artistic excellence, cultural exchange, and meaningful public engagement. We believe Rangoli is more than decorative art — it is a universal language that brings people together, celebrates diversity, and creates unforgettable cultural experiences.
